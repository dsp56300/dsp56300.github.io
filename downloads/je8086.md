## Performance test

Find out whether your computer is fast enough before you install. Unpack it, put the firmware into the same folder and run `jeTestConsole` on its own. It plays the factory demo song. A result of 100% means that you can most likely run one instance of the emulator per CPU core, 200% two, and so on. Leave room for your DAW and your other plugins: this is an estimate, not a promise.
