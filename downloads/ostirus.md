## Performance test

Find out whether your computer is fast enough before you install. Unpack it, put the firmware into the same folder and run `virusTestConsole`. It plays the factory demo song. The Virus TI runs two DSPs at 133 MHz each and needs a combined result of 266 MIPS or more. The Snow has one DSP at 163 MHz and needs 163 MIPS; the test cannot run in Snow mode yet.
